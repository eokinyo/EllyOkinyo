Research interests

Labour markets, especially how downturns hit different groups (in Finland, people of foreign background have faced much higher unemployment than the rest of the population). Remittances and household finance, with a focus on Kenya's diaspora inflows and what drives them. Time-series econometrics and forecasting: volatility models, forecast evaluation, and when machine learning helps and when it doesn't.

How I work

  • Method first. I choose the model to fit the question and the data, benchmark it against something simple, and report when the fancy option doesn't win.
  • Reproducible by default. Code, data steps and outputs live in version control so anyone can rerun every table, as with my thesis repo.
  • Plain-language results. I write up what the numbers mean for a decision, with the uncertainty stated, not just the coefficients.

MSc thesis · 2026

Does Google Trends Improve Bitcoin Volatility Forecasts?

University of Helsinki (Research Track) · Supervised by Prof. Mika Meitz

Master's thesis, University of Helsinki (Research Track), supervised by Prof. Mika Meitz. Compares GARCH(1,1) and IGARCH(1,1) under normal and Student-t innovations, with and without lagged Google Trends attention, against Random Forest regression. Daily data from Jan 2017 to Sep 2025, QLIKE loss, SPA and MCS tests. Result: Gaussian GARCH(1,1) was not significantly outperformed, and sentiment added little. Fully reproducible in R + Python.
